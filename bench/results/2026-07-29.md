# Benchmark snapshot — 2026-07-29

Same-machine results from a 16 GB Apple M1 Pro MacBook Pro (10 cores), macOS
15.5. Query times are medians in milliseconds; lower is better.

## Transactions

The [write harness](../write_bench.kvist) inserts entities with a long and a
string attribute. Throughput is entities per second; higher is better. Vev
results are medians of three runs.

| Entities/tx | Datoms/tx | Entities | Datomic in-memory | Vev in-memory |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 2 | 10,000 | 14,620 | 5,538 |
| 10 | 20 | 10,000 | 49,673 | 33,471 |
| 100 | 200 | 10,000 | 58,340 | 51,911 |
| 1,000 | 2,000 | 10,000 | 50,830 | 52,956 |

Vev is ahead of Datomic at 1,000 entities per transaction and reaches 89% of
Datomic's throughput at 100. Small transactions remain the in-memory gap.

The durable comparison uses the same transaction sizes in Vev,
[Datomic](../write_bench/run_datomic.sh), and Datalevin's
`benchmarks/write-bench`. Transaction construction is outside the timed
region. Vev and Datomic are medians of three runs with the Datomic transactor
already warm.

| Entities/tx | Datoms/tx | Entities | Datomic durable | Datalevin durable | Vev durable |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 2 | 100 | 391 | 199 | 1,306 |
| 10 | 20 | 1,000 | 3,096 | 1,932 | 6,664 |
| 100 | 200 | 10,000 | 21,061 | 15,394 | 17,667 |
| 1,000 | 2,000 | 10,000 | 22,113 | 55,845 | 20,839 |

Vev leads Datomic at 1 and 10 entities per transaction, reaches 84% of
Datomic's throughput at 100, and 94% at 1,000. Datalevin remains substantially
faster at 1,000 entities per transaction.
